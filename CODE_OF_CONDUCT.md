# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and considerate
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experience levels
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally
- Focusing discussions on the project and its users
- Taking responsibility, apologising, and correcting mistakes

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberately disruptive conduct
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and other public interactions made on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer using the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/krutharth-dev). Do not post sensitive details publicly.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour violates this Code of Conduct.
